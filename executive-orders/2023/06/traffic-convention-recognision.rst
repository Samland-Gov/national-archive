2023 Traffic Convention Recognition
===================================

:executive-order-date: 2023-06-06
:executive-order-author: @samuelh2005

The President hereby recognises:
1. the informal practices used by Samland's citizens and residents in relation to traffic and road usage, and
2. general worldwide traffic regulations and conventions.

On the basis of this recognition, the following traffic rules are hereby established in Samland:

* Driving on the left of a road's central reservation, unless road markings or signage indicate otherwise.
* Using Kilometres per hour (km/h) as the standard unit of speed measurement.
* Going clockwise around roundabouts, unless road markings or signage indicate otherwise.
* Using the metric system for all road signage, including speed limits, distances, and other relevant information.
