2026 New National Archive
=========================

:executive-order-date: 2026-08-12
:executive-order-author: @samuelh2005

The President has ordered the creation of a new National Archive, which will be tasked with the following:

* Preserving and maintaining all historical documents and records of the nation.
* Providing access to these documents for research and educational purposes.
* Organising and cataloging the documents to ensure they are easily accessible and searchable.

The President shall submit reconstructed executive orders and significant pre 2024 legislation to the National Archive for preservation.
