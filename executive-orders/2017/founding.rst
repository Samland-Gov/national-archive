Founding
========

:executive-order-date: 2017
:executive-order-author: @samuelh2005

It is now declared that the country of Samland is founded, and that Samland
is ruled by `@samuelh2005 <https://github.com/samuelh2005>`__ as an
absolute President.

The President is the head of state and government, and is responsible for the
administration of the country.

Samland shall exclude any form of democracy, and the President shall have
absolute power over all aspects of the country.

The President is also the commander-in-chief of the armed forces, and has
absolute authority over the executive, legislative, and judicial branches
of government.
