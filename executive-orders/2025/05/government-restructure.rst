2025 Government Restructure
===========================

:executive-order-date: 2025-05-21
:executive-order-author: @samuelh2005

The President hereby orders a restructure of the government of Samland, introducing
two new departments, including the Department for Resource Management and
the Department for Planning and Development. Both shall be ruled by the President.

Department for Resource Management
----------------------------------

The Department for Resource Management is tasked with overseeing 
resource assessments, acquisition and logistics, in addition to
allocating resources to various state projects.

Department for Planning and Development
---------------------------------------

The Department for Planning and Development is tasked with overseeing
land use planning, architectural design standards, project management
of state projects, and infrastructure development.
