2025 Department Disbanding
==========================

:executive-order-date: 2025-08-23
:executive-order-author: @samuelh2005

After 3 months of operation, the President had decided to disband the the Department
for Resource Management and the Department for Planning and Development.

This decision was made as creating two new departments was deemed unnecessary
and inefficient, as the departments actually did not take on any new responsibilities
as they were still ruled by the President.
