2025 Government Restructure
===========================

On May 21, 2025, two new government departments were established,
the Department for Resource Management and the Department for
Planning and Development.

Department for Resource Management
----------------------------------

The Department for Resource Management was established to oversee 
resource assessments, acquisition and logistics, in addition to
allocating resources to various state projects.

This department was later disbanded on August 23, 2025.

Department for Planning and Development
---------------------------------------

The Department for Planning and Development was responsible for overseeing
land use planning, architectural design standards, project management
of state projects, and infrastructure development.

This department was also disbanded on August 23, 2025.
