Founding
========

The nation of Samland was founded in 2017 by a single individual
`@samuelh2005 <https://github.com/samuelh2005>`, who became the
absolute President of Samland.

The exact date of the founding is unknown, but it is believed to
have occurred sometime between 2017 and 2018.

Later on, Samland established sovereignty within Minecraft on
the `Miners Online <https://www.minersonline.uk>` platform, of
which is also ruled by the same individual.
